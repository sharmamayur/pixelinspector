Pixel Inspector by PixelMonitor: privacy policy

Updated September 19, 2026. This policy covers the Pixel Inspector Chrome extension.

What inspection records

When you open the side panel or switch to a website tab while the panel is open, the extension records page origins and paths, navigation, clicks, clicked-element labels and selectors, and form-submission events in that tab. It also records recognized advertising and analytics requests, including vendor and destination identifiers, event names, request payload fields, timestamps, and browser delivery outcomes. Browser version information is included in the session.

Marketing payloads can contain personal identifiers, customer data, page URLs, location information, transaction details, and other sensitive information sent by the website. Pixel Inspector does not read browser cookies or request headers, capture screenshots, or record keystrokes or form-field values directly. Such information may nevertheless appear in the website's marketing payloads. Inspect only websites and data you are authorized to inspect.

Purpose and controls

This data is used only to show the visitor journey alongside the marketing requests observed afterward and to create exports you request. Inspection starts automatically for the selected website tab. Recording follows that tab through navigation, including to other websites, and continues if you switch tabs or close the side panel. Pause stops capture; Resume continues it. Clear deletes the captured results and immediately starts a fresh recording. Use Pause to stop capture. The toolbar badge shows REC while recording.

Storage and deletion

Inspection data is stored locally in Chrome session storage. It is removed when you clear the session, close its tab, or end the browser session. Disabling, reloading, or uninstalling the extension also clears Chrome session storage. Downloads are separate local files and remain until you delete them yourself.

Sharing and transfers

The extension does not upload captured data to PixelMonitor or any other recipient. It contains no usage analytics, advertising, or remote code. It does not sell inspection data, use it for advertising, or use it for credit or lending decisions. PixelMonitor cannot read your locally stored inspection sessions.

Export buttons save HTML, JSON, or a plain-text inspection summary to your device; they do not send messages. Review files for sensitive information before choosing to share them. Websites you inspect independently transmit their own requests under their own privacy practices; Pixel Inspector observes those requests without initiating them.

Opening a website link or choosing Send feedback is a separate action. Feedback opens your email application; captured inspection data is not attached automatically. Include only information you intend to share with support.

Limited Use

Pixel Inspector's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Inspection data is used solely for the extension's disclosed inspection and export features.
